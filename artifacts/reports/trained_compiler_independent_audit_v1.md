# Independent trained-compiler audit — 2 October 2026

**Decision:** a trained selector exists; a useful learned-method contribution remains unestablished. Do not advance to fresh confirmation unless direct frozen-backbone development shows a material advantage over the fixed heuristic and equally informed learned controls.

**Novelty.** Neural recommendation of feature transformations from dataset summaries predates this project ([Nargesian et al., IJCAI 2017](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2017/352)); permutation-invariant set encoders are established ([Deep Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06114)). Learned nonlinear use of missingness indicators also has substantial precedent ([NeuMiss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01627)). The cited [Mamba/ICL study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04248) explicitly studies sparse parity learning. The potentially distinctive combination is support-conditioned selection of a reversible mask basis for a frozen predictor. Parity, invertibility, amortized transformation selection, and training an MLP are individually insufficient novelty claims. This targeted search cannot guarantee novelty or exhaust the literature.

**Independent CPU evidence.** Audited all 3,584 saved predictions on 512 development tasks: every recorded prediction hash, expected/empirical/oracle NLL, aggregate score, basis mapping, teacher match and active-parity diagnostic agrees. Maximum score discrepancy is zero. Independent NumPy inference reproduces every neural and heuristic decision; confidence differences are at most 7.75e-7. Prototype and heuristic select identical bases and issue exactly identical probabilities on all 256 gamma=.9 tasks. At gamma=0, the prototype chooses identity on all 256 tasks; the heuristic differs on one task. This is evidence of one avoided heuristic selection error, without evidence of a learned advantage under signal. Details: `trained_compiler_independent_scores_v1.json`. Estimator fits were not independently rerun.

**Fairness.** Prototype, linear and DeepSets receive identical synthetic tasks, privileged active/gamma teachers, optimizer, epochs and minibatches, with fixed final checkpoints. DeepSets and prototype each have 6,092 parameters; linear has 216. However, the prototype receives 15 engineered absolute parity/label moments, whereas DeepSets must learn interactions from rows. Equal update counts do not guarantee equally suitable optimization or converged competitors. The prototype is a learned selector over fixed moments, not a learned raw-observation encoder. CV searches 12 bases using 108 fits; full-parity L1 has 15 mask features versus four. Neither computational nor feature budgets are fully equivalent. Selection accuracy does not optimize backbone loss; confidence=.8 provides no no-harm guarantee.

**Boundaries and natural data.** Source inspection separates labeled support from query inputs and evaluator targets; privileged teachers enter pretraining only. Query labels inform offline stratification/scoring, not selector inference or backbone fitting. Natural columns rank solely by support missing rates. The [Hepatitis](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/46/hepatitis) and [Horse Colic](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/47/horse+colic) ingestion preserves native missing values and outcomes: 155 and 353 canonical rows. Grouped splits isolate supplied IDs/identical covariates but cannot establish unknown patient independence. Historical treatment/timing, pooled Horse splits, merged death/euthanasia, and numeric nominal codes limit interpretation. The selector uses only four masks with a zero U proxy, a distribution shift. Three folds per dataset share training rows; two datasets do not support population-generalization intervals. Native absence does not identify MCAR/MAR/MNAR. No prospective, causal, clinical, contemporary-checkpoint, universal-TFM or no-harm claim is supported.

Direct GPU predictions are pending this audit. A transient runner-directory defect caused two of 15 independent mock tests to fail; the owning agent is repairing it before freezing. Passing tests and artifact checks cannot guarantee absence of every leakage or implementation defect.
