Our elementary fitting-design projection is not a double-machine-learning theorem, a causal estimator or established new methodology. The negative experiment narrows the failure mechanism: merely changing the correction basis and fallback does not produce native utility from these synthetic-trained checkpoints. It does not prove that native meta-training will solve the problem.
