## Related work and unresolved novelty

[Learning to Reweight Examples](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/ren18a.html) uses validation guidance in meta-learning. [Meta-Weight-Net](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/e58cc5ca94270acaceed13bc82dfedf7-Abstract.html) learns an explicit weighting function. [MetaOptNet](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Lee_Meta-Learning_With_Differentiable_Convex_Optimization_CVPR_2019_paper.html) provides a differentiable-optimization foundation. Thus validation-guided weighting, learned gates and differentiable solvers are established ideas. The candidate claim is a demonstrably useful query-specific missingness correction operator with support-only verification and explicit label costs. Cross-domain efficacy, comparison to contemporary missing-data methods, direct novelty review, calibration and larger native confirmation remain required.
