# A concrete extension of three related papers

October 4, 2026. Recommendation: a **support-conditioned Bellman risk model**, combining L2M's adaptation from labeled support examples with BRiG-AFA's delayed-value objective. This is an implementable research extension, not an established novelty claim or a promise to fix every limitation.

| Primary evidence | Explicit scope or limitation |
|---|---|
| [BRiG-AFA v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02305v1), Sections 4 and 7 | Fits separate budget-specific risk networks for a task. Authors explicitly report compounded bootstrap error, action-enumeration cost, a predictor fitted on random rather than induced masks, unit costs/fixed horizons, fully observed training, and insufficient broad matched comparisons. |
| [AFA-ITD v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32325v1), Sections 3–5 and Appendix B | Theory assumes MCAR, finite discrete features, bounded loss, and a fixed independently learned predictor. Experiments fit the predictor before artificial missingness; restoration does not uniformly help. Already includes heterogeneous costs and availability-conditioned Q networks. Thus cost/availability conditioning is not its missing capability. |
| [L2M preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12624v1), Sections 4 and 6; [ICML 2026 final record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/kobayashi26a.html) | The preprint trains a support-conditioned greedy one-step objective with blocked actions and joint predictor/policy fitting. Its explicit limitations are sufficient offline action coverage and MAR, limited pretraining-prior breadth, and time-invariant settings. The final abstract confirms in-context acquisition without per-task retraining. Final PDF retrieval failed; preprint details above are not asserted as verified final-version limitations. |

**Proposed mechanism.** Encode a labeled support set of partially observed rows with a permutation-invariant network. Combine that encoding with the query's observed values/mask, legal continuation set, remaining horizon, and candidate identity. Train a shared predictor plus horizon-specific risk heads for horizons 0–3 across source tasks. For candidate acquisition, regress the realized next-state minimum of stopping risk and shorter-horizon action risks; learn stopping risk from query-label loss. Fit shorter horizons first with frozen target copies. Deployment uses one checkpoint and target-task support labels, with no gradient updates or query labels. Use fixed unit costs initially; cost conditioning is unnecessary for the first mechanism test.

This targets two specific gaps: delayed acquisitions beyond L2M's inspected greedy objective, and cross-task support adaptation without BRiG-style task-specific risk-network refitting. A third implementation improvement is to refit the predictor on source-policy visited masks; after changing it, regenerate risk targets from horizon zero upward. Joint training itself is already in L2M and is not claimed novel.

**Incomplete-data boundary.** On each training query row, construct targets only for measured candidates and legal continuation sets entirely contained in that row's observed columns. Include that set in Q's input: omitting it aliases a restricted Bellman target with the unrestricted problem. At deployment, use a predetermined support-coverage gate and stop when required continuation coverage fails. This is conservative scope restriction, not recovery of unobserved information. Arbitrary MNAR, absent overlap, and unseen task priors cannot be universally repaired from the same observations; generative guesses do not establish identification. Time-varying acquisition remains out of scope.

**Decisive controls and gate.** Split task generators/families before fitting; use fresh tasks and fixed support sizes. Compare the identical support encoder/predictor with one-step heads, support-blinded multi-step heads, and task-specific BRiG with explicitly reported fitting cost. Include a same-belief direct planner; exact-source oracles are diagnostic only. Equalize source labels, model capacity where possible, and measured compute. Require a frozen realized loss-plus-cost margin over the strongest matched control across three seeds, plus better delayed-value action choices on held-out complementary-feature tasks. Kill the extension if gains vanish with matched predictor/support information or arise only from additional supervision. Passing establishes useful integration; broader prior-art review and independent real-data evidence remain necessary.
