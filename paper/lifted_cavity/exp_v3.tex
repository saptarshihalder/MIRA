\subsection{Lifted sites as a transformer output layer (protocol v3)}
\label{sec:exp-v3}

Protocol v2 left a split: structure wins on synthetic tasks, the transformer on real networks after fine-tuning. The
\emph{lifted cavity transformer} (LCT) keeps the transformer's backbone, recipe and seed and replaces only its output
head: a zero-initialized linear map turns each observed query cell's final token into the correction
$(\delta^{\mathrm{off}}_j,\delta^{\mathrm{slope}}_j,\delta^{D}_j,s_j)$ of that sensor's site in \eqref{eq:relin},
linearized at the FA-Gaussian posterior mean, and the prediction is the $y$-marginal of the product of observed sites.
Missing sensors contribute no site and their readings never enter; at initialization the LCT is its anchor's closed
form. Because the hypothesis was formed after the v2 results, v3 uses only new data, two fresh synthetic panels (G1,
five sensors; G3, sixteen) and two Beijing pollutants never scored before (NO$_2$ and CO), and its four endpoints were
committed before any of these existed (Table~\ref{tab:endpoints}).
