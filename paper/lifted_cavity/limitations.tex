\textbf{Scope.} Exact oracle and sign-conditioned HMC references exist only for our generator (one-factor noise, sinusoidal
nonlinearity, Gaussian targets). Per-sensor dropout is simulated on Air Quality and mostly simulated on Beijing,
whose natural missingness covers \Rbeijingnatmiss\% of query readings; the gas-drift benchmark could not separate
methods (Appendix~\ref{app:audit}). \textbf{Model class.} Gaussian sites cannot represent multimodal posteriors; we
used $K=1$ throughout. \textbf{Baselines.} The reported transformers use 203k parameters and 30{,}000 steps;
second seeds reproduce the synthetic comparisons, but the LCT's real-data gain varies with the seed. A three-seed
2.1M-parameter GPU study completed, but missing checkpoint bindings blocked independent verification
(Appendix~\ref{app:audit}). Its endpoints and pretrained TabPFN~v2 efficacy remain unverified; TabICL is unevaluated.
