## Numeric preservation rule

`generated/numbers.tex` is copied without alteration from the historical paper. Table edits in this manuscript are limited to wording, typography and column layout. Numeric body tokens and endpoint decisions must match their historical table source, with mathematical minus signs retained. Entries formerly displayed as double dash are rendered as `n/a`; v4 status is explained as unverified and withheld. Any new future scientific analysis needs its own identifiable protocol and cannot replace these records silently.
