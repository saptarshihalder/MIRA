Missing sensors call for structure, and the structure is unique: products of per-sensor sites are the free
construction on sensors, lifting them to a target-plus-nuisance latent makes them exact under shared noise, and the
linear opinion pool combines them with flexible models without losing what either does well. The Universal Pool
Transformer realizes this structure in one network, and the same pool turns a pretrained foundation model into a
predictor that is sharp when sensors are present and robust when they fail. Under protocols committed before their
data were scored, UPT\,$\oplus$\,TabPFN improved TabPFN v2 on every one of four networks recorded in 2026, and also
improved the much stronger TabPFN-3.5; UPT was the best partner for both. A richer pretraining prior would close the
remaining gap of UPT alone on near-deterministic targets.
