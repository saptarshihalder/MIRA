Missing sensors call for structure, and the structure is unique:
\begin{itemize}
  \item products of per-sensor sites are the free construction on sensors;
  \item lifting the sites to a target-plus-nuisance latent makes them exact under shared noise;
  \item the linear opinion pool combines them with flexible models without losing what either does well.
\end{itemize}
The Universal Pool Transformer realizes this structure in one network. Through the same pool, it turns a pretrained
foundation model into a predictor that is sharp when sensors are present and robust when they fail. Under protocols
committed before their data were scored, UPT\,$\oplus$\,TabPFN improved TabPFN v2 on every one of four sensor networks
recorded in 2026, and more as inputs failed. UPT was the best partner. The next steps are a richer pretraining prior,
which would close the remaining gap of UPT alone on near-deterministic targets, and pooling with newer foundation
models.
