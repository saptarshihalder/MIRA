Beyond the failures reported in \S\ref{sec:results}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cost.} UPT\,$\oplus$\,TabPFN runs both models, plus two half-support cross-fit calls per episode for
  the stacking weight.
  \item \textbf{Baselines.} TabPFN v2 (all networks) and TabPFN-3.5 (the 2026 networks) run in context without
  fine-tuning; neither is given the training history that UPT is fine-tuned on. Our kriging baseline is a
  local-graph adaptation, not a full-graph temporal model (GRIN~\citep{cini2022grin},
  graph neural processes~\citep{hu2023stgnp}), which may do better with a long, stationary history.
  \item \textbf{Data.} Episodes ignore temporal order within a window. The 2025--2026 data are provisional; we
  release content digests and download code. Protocols v12 and v13 reuse the v10 networks, and TabPFN-3.5 was
  released at the end of their test periods, so its training data may overlap with them.
\end{itemize}
